Due Diligence

Sanctions, PEP, adverse media, court cases, and enforcement findings. Review and adjudicate signals here.

Compliance notice. Findings are automatically aggregated from open data sources (OFAC, EU, UN, UK HMT, OpenSanctions, GDELT, NewsAPI, SEC EDGAR, CourtListener, UK Companies House) and matched heuristically — including AI-assisted arbitration on ambiguous hits. They are not legal advice and may contain false positives. A human reviewer must confirm any finding before it informs a contracting, KYC, or onboarding decision. Adverse-media items reflect press coverage, not adjudicated wrongdoing.
