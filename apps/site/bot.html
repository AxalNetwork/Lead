AxalVCBot/1.0

AxalVCBot/1.0 (+https://axal.vc/bot) is the public web-crawler operated by Axal Network on behalf of the AI Data Signal platform. It reads publicly available pages to keep our profile data fresh — investors, lawyers, founders, journalists, public-sector organizations, and similar professional profiles.

What we do

  • Fetch only public pages over HTTPS, identifying ourselves with the User-Agent above.
  • Respect robots.txt for both User-agent: * and User-agent: AxalVCBot directives, including Crawl-delay.
  • Cap our request rate at roughly one request every two seconds per host by default.
  • On 429 or 503 we back off exponentially and quarantine the host for up to one hour.
  • Cache robots.txt for 24 hours.

What we don't do

  • We do not log into any service, submit forms, or attempt to bypass paywalls or authentication.
  • We do not crawl content marked non-public, gated, or otherwise off-limits by your site.
  • We do not use commercial proxy services or rotate identities to defeat blocks.
  • We do not collect personal data beyond what is already public on a professional profile page.

How to block us

Add the following to /robots.txt:

User-agent: AxalVCBot
Disallow: /

To restrict us to specific directories:

User-agent: AxalVCBot
Disallow: /admin/
Disallow: /private/
Crawl-delay: 10

We honor these directives within 24 hours of the cache TTL expiring.
