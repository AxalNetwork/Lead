Task 12 — Suggested additional data points & sources to capture
This is a continuous backlog. Integrate progressively into Tasks 3, 4, and 6.
Data points worth adding (beyond Task 3's schema):
Education: institutions, degrees, graduation years (drives age inference).
Employment history with start/end dates (drives seniority and tenure).
Patents (patents.google.com) — deep tech signal.
Publications / citations (scholar.google.com, orcid.org).
Board memberships (public-company DEF 14A filings via SEC EDGAR).
Government roles (USAspending.gov, EU Transparency Register).
Political donations (FEC.gov, OpenSecrets) — geopolitical context.
Real-estate ownership signals (county assessor APIs, ATTOM, where legal).
Speaking engagements (conference attendee + speaker pages).
Podcast appearances (Listen Notes API).
News mentions (GDELT, NewsAPI, MediaCloud).
Sentiment / influence score (followers, engagement rate from public profiles).
Co-investor graph (Crunchbase + SEC Form D).
Time-to-respond statistics (after campaigns run).
Calendar availability hints (public Calendly / Cal.com pages — opt-in only).

VC & startup: Crunchbase, PitchBook (if licensed), CB Insights, Tracxn, Dealroom, AngelList, Signal NFX, NFX Marketplace, eu-startups.com.
